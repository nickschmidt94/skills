# Brainstorm

Use this branch to generate small-product directions grounded in a real problem, audience, or portfolio gap.

## Required evidence

Account for:

- a problem the user or a reachable customer actually experiences;
- evidence of frequency, severity, or willingness to pay;
- the audience and how the user can reach it;
- existing products, capabilities, and reusable distribution;
- time, maintenance, lifestyle, and risk constraints.

Research repeated complaints when the user asks for current market evidence. Otherwise distinguish reported pain from inferred demand.

## Apply the lens

1. **Own itch:** identify a specific recurring job, workaround, or cost. Personal pain is evidence of one user, not proof of a market.
2. **Audience pain:** connect the problem to observed or reported audience behavior. Mark pattern-matching without current evidence as a hypothesis.
3. **Portfolio gap:** identify a missing capability or customer step that would make an existing product more useful.
4. **Free-tool play:** consider a free acquisition tool only when it attracts the same audience and has a concrete path to a paid product or portfolio asset.
5. **Smallest test:** define the fastest responsible test of the riskiest assumption. Estimate build time only from inspected scope or explicit assumptions.
6. **Solo and lifestyle fit:** include maintenance, support, and recurring obligations in the idea quality.
7. **Completion mechanism:** give each idea a measurable validation threshold and a stop or pivot rule.

## Output contract

Offer up to three sufficiently distinct ideas, ordered by evidence and leverage rather than forced labels. For each, provide:

- the user and paid outcome;
- the evidence and material assumption;
- the smallest validation test;
- monetization path;
- portfolio connection;
- maintenance and lifestyle cost;
- validation threshold and stop rule.

Then provide:

- **Recommended direction** — one choice, or no-build when the evidence does not support a product.
- **Free-tool option** — only when it creates a credible funnel or portfolio asset.
- **Unknowns and assumptions** — only those material to the recommendation.
- **Next actions** — up to three smallest supported actions.

This branch is complete when every proposed idea has a real user, paid outcome, evidence label, smallest test, economics, portfolio path, operating cost, and completion rule.
