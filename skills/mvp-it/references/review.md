# Review

Use this branch to evaluate an existing product or plan as a sustainable solo business.

## Required evidence

Account for:

- the user's goal for the product;
- current revenue and trend, or the intended monetization model;
- operating costs and maintenance effort;
- support burden and customer obligations;
- deployment and iteration speed;
- acquisition and retention signals that are actually available;
- distribution channels and portfolio relationships.

Use `unknown` instead of projecting unsupported revenue targets or numerical scores.

## Apply the lens

1. **Revenue reality:** describe the current economics and the next plausible paid milestone. Model larger targets only when price, conversion, market, and capacity assumptions are available.
2. **Complexity:** identify complexity that creates customer value, complexity required for trust, and accidental complexity. Recommend subtracting only the third category.
3. **Flywheel:** trace actual cross-promotion or customer movement. Call the product an island when no credible path exists.
4. **Distribution story:** assess build-in-public as one possible channel. A quiet or unshareable product can still win through another evidenced channel.
5. **Support calculus:** compare support effort and obligations with revenue and strategic value. Free-user support policy remains a product choice.
6. **Iteration speed:** identify the narrowest bottleneck preventing a safe same-day experiment, if one exists.
7. **Lifestyle and completion:** test whether the product fits the user's desired operating life and has a clear next decision point.

## Output contract

Provide:

- **Verdict** — direct assessment with confidence proportional to evidence.
- **Economics** — current state and next plausible paid milestone.
- **Complexity** — the main avoidable burden; use a numerical score only when its basis is explained.
- **Flywheel** — connected, speculative, or island, with the path shown.
- **Distribution** — strongest evidenced channel and whether public building fits.
- **Support and iteration** — healthy, constrained, or unknown, with the reason.
- **Subtractions** — only changes that preserve the product's core value and safeguards.
- **Monetization move** — one supported experiment when appropriate.
- **Unknowns and assumptions** — only those material to the verdict.
- **Next actions** — up to three smallest supported actions.

This branch is complete when economics, complexity, flywheel, distribution, support, iteration speed, lifestyle fit, completion mechanism, and material unknowns are all addressed.
