# Ship

Use this branch to reduce a concrete product or active build to the smallest responsible sellable launch.

## Required evidence

Account for:

- the user and painful job being served;
- the single outcome they would pay to obtain;
- current implementation state;
- the payment or commitment mechanism;
- a distribution channel with an evidenced audience fit;
- material trust, legal, privacy, reliability, or support constraints;
- relevant portfolio connections.

Mark missing items unknown. A launch date or build-time estimate must be reported by the user, observed from the work, or labeled as an assumption.

## Apply the lens

1. **One outcome:** express the minimum sellable product in one sentence. One end-to-end customer outcome can require more than one technical component.
2. **Scope machete:** separate launch-critical work from exclusions. Recommend parking or hiding excluded work; removal requires explicit authority.
3. **Payment test:** identify the smallest real commitment that validates value. A low price is one option, not a universal rule.
4. **Risk floor:** retain the minimum safeguards required for trust and responsible operation. Speed does not waive them.
5. **Launch test:** choose only channels whose audience and posting norms fit the product. Treat publishing and outreach as proposed actions until authorized.
6. **Flywheel:** name a concrete traffic, trust, data, or customer path between this product and the portfolio. Mark a speculative connection as inferred.
7. **Completion mechanism:** define an observable launch gate and a date or decision rule for ship, revise, or stop.

## Output contract

Provide:

- **Verdict** — whether the product is ready to reduce and ship, conditionally ready, or missing decisive evidence.
- **One outcome** — one-sentence sellable value.
- **Launch-critical scope** — only what must work end to end.
- **Out of launch scope** — what to defer, hide, or preserve without active work.
- **Commitment test** — payment or another meaningful customer commitment.
- **Launch gate** — observable conditions for “done enough.”
- **Distribution** — evidence-backed targets and why they fit.
- **Portfolio fit** — concrete connection or “island.”
- **Unknowns and assumptions** — only those material to the verdict.
- **Next actions** — up to three smallest supported actions.

This branch is complete when the sellable outcome, launch gate, commitment test, exclusions, constraints, distribution, flywheel, and material unknowns are all accounted for.
