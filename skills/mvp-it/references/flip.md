# Flip

Use this branch to decide whether to keep, retire, pivot, or sell an existing product.

## Required evidence

Account for:

- revenue and customer trend over a meaningful available period;
- costs, maintenance hours, support burden, and contractual obligations;
- user dependence, data handling, and migration duties;
- portfolio contribution and opportunity cost;
- transferable assets, liabilities, and sale readiness;
- the user's financial and lifestyle goals.

Current valuation ranges require current marketplace evidence. Avoid cached SaaS multiples; when evidence is unavailable, leave valuation unknown and explain what would determine it.

## Apply the lens

1. **Trajectory:** distinguish growth, flatness, decline, seasonality, and insufficient evidence. Do not infer a trend from one period.
2. **Effort economics:** calculate effective return only from known revenue, costs, and time. Include strategic value separately rather than hiding it in the arithmetic.
3. **Portfolio drag:** identify whether the product supplies revenue, audience, trust, data, capability, or distraction.
4. **Pivot test:** propose a pivot only when one bounded change tests a supported hypothesis within a defined window.
5. **Sale readiness:** assess transferable revenue, code, domain, brand, traffic, documentation, customer concentration, liabilities, and owner dependence.
6. **Responsible retirement:** treat “kill” as an orderly retirement or archive plan. Preserve user data and code, honor obligations, and obtain authority before deletion or customer communication.
7. **Completion mechanism:** define the metric, date, and threshold that resolves keep, pivot, retire, or sell.

## Output contract

Provide:

- **Verdict** — KEEP, RETIRE, PIVOT, SELL, or INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE.
- **Evidence** — trajectory, effort economics, support burden, and portfolio contribution.
- **Rationale** — why this option beats the alternatives under the user's goals.
- **Valuation** — evidence-backed range with assumptions, or unknown.
- **Portfolio impact and opportunity cost** — concrete effects of keeping or removing the product.
- **Transition** — reversible experiment, retirement safeguards, or sale-readiness work as applicable.
- **Decision rule** — dated or threshold-based completion mechanism.
- **Unknowns and assumptions** — only those material to the verdict.
- **Next actions** — up to three smallest supported actions.

This branch is complete when trajectory, effort economics, support, obligations, portfolio impact, opportunity cost, transition path, valuation basis, decision rule, and material unknowns are all accounted for.
